AmbWave

An ambient instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1. Hold a chord, shape its movement, add space.

Version 0.1.0 · Based on Felucca

Four tracks. Four controls.

TIDE, TEXTURE, CLOUD and scattered plucks. SHAPE, MOTION, DENSITY and SPACE. Direct mixing, held chords and deliberate phrases.

By mentalcells · Try TIDE with Space 0 to hear the dry FM source. Browser audio starts only after you click Switch on. Demo saves stay in this browser.

Hardware validation

Maintainer-tested on an FM-1: browser installation, startup, playback, controls and normal restart. Rollback and interrupted-update recovery remain untested on hardware.

MVP limits

Eight shared voices, shared effects and no complete scene save/recall. Dense layering can steal voices. The browser does not emulate hardware USB, TRS MIDI or user sample slots.
